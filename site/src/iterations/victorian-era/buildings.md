# Buildings

See the [buildings overview](../../buildings.md) for submissions, passive and active buildings, and production timing.

## Passive buildings

Passive buildings add resources to your town's income every hour.

### Coal factory

| Tier | Coal per hour |
| :--: | --: |
| 1 | 100 |
| 2 | 200 |
| 3 | 300 |

### Farm

Farms produce food.

| Tier | Food per hour |
| :--: | :-- |
| 1 | 1 Wheat, 1 Beef |
| 2 | 2 Wheat, 2 Beef, 1 Carrot |
| 3 | 3 Wheat, 3 Beef, 2 Carrots, 1 Potato |

### Port

Ports produce fish and let you teleport to any port within range owned by your town, your nation or an ally, or to a public port. Range depends on the tier of the port you leave from. Ports must be built within 2 chunks of your territory's edge.

| Tier | Teleport range | Fish per hour |
| :--: | --: | :-- |
| 1 | 2500 blocks | 1 Cod |
| 2 | 5000 blocks | 2 Cod, 1 Salmon |
| 3 | 10000 blocks | 3 Cod, 2 Salmon, 1 Tropical Fish |

### Train station

Train stations produce a small amount of coal. Rails connect stations for fast travel between them.

| Tier | Rail speed | Coal per hour |
| :--: | --: | --: |
| 1 | 25 bps | 8 |
| 2 | 50 bps | 16 |
| 3 | 100 bps | 32 |

## Active buildings

Factories turn coal into field pieces, boats and air vehicles. Each item takes **3 hours** to produce. At higher tiers you choose which item to produce, and everything unlocked at a lower tier stays available. Costs are in coal; a dash means the item is not available at that tier.

::: warning Raidable
Finished items drop on the ground at the factory and never despawn. Anyone who reaches the factory can take them, so defend it.
:::
