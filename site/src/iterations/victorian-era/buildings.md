# Buildings

See the [buildings overview](../../buildings.md) for submissions, passive and active buildings, and production timing.

## Passive buildings

Passive buildings add resources to your town's income every hour.

### Coal factory

| Tier | Coal per hour |
| :--: | --: |
| 1 | 100 |
| 2 | 200 |
| 3 | 300 |

## Active buildings

Factories turn coal into boats and field pieces. Each item takes **3 hours** to produce. At higher tiers you choose which item to produce, and everything unlocked at a lower tier stays available. Costs are in coal; a dash means the item is not available at that tier.

::: warning Raidable
Finished items drop on the ground at the factory and never despawn. Anyone who reaches the factory can take them, so defend it.
:::
